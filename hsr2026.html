Overview

Humanoid Soccer Robots Workshop 2026

Advancing the future of autonomous humanoid robotics through soccer

Robot soccer is a uniquely demanding benchmark for humanoid robotics. This workshop connects the RoboCup community with the broader humanoid robotics, robot learning, control, and embodied-AI communities to examine how these capabilities can be advanced jointly.

Expected Attendance: ~50 participants from academia and industry

Participation & Visibility

Target Audience: Roboticists, AI researchers, machine learning engineers, control systems designers, and embodied intelligence experts from academia and industry.

Inclusion & Diversity

The workshop is committed to fostering an inclusive research community with balanced gender representation, diverse scientific expertise, and support for researchers at all career stages. All content will be accessible on mobile devices and shared asynchronously through dedicated platforms (Discord, website).
